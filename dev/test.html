On Android or a computer

Use your browser's Install app or Add to Home Screen option. If this browser offers an install button below, you can use it.

Open the calendar online once and wait for Ready offline in the footer. Then you can revisit the missions and your saved creations without internet. Planning with ChatGPT still needs an internet connection.

Keep your inventions safe

Keep your original HTML files in Files or Downloads. Browser storage isn't a permanent backup, and a different device or browser has its own stars and creations.
